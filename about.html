Sr. Staff Designer / Senior Manager of Product Design — Chicago, IL

"Design is about creating systems that empower people — not just making things look good."

Danny Kaufman is a senior design leader with over a decade of experience building product design teams and shipping experiences used by millions of people. He currently operates as Sr. Staff Designer and Senior Manager of Product Design, leading both IC craft and a growing team of designers across multiple product surfaces.

His approach to design is rooted in systems thinking — understanding that the best products emerge from clear principles, rigorous process, and genuine curiosity about people's needs. He's equally comfortable in the weeds of interaction details as he is presenting a product vision to executive stakeholders.

Before his current role, Danny held senior and staff design positions at companies across fintech, productivity, and enterprise software — contributing to products at various stages from zero-to-one to mature platform scale. He has mentored dozens of designers and helped build design culture in organizations where design was still finding its footing.

Outside of work, Danny is interested in typography, modernist graphic design history, open-source tools, and the occasional hardware project. He lives in Chicago with his family.
